# Reproducibility map — AMMBEC Denver urban-methane paper

Every quantitative claim in the manuscript is regenerated by the R pipeline in
this repository. Run from the **project root** (the directory holding `config.R`):

```bash
export METHANE_DATA_DIR="$HOME/MethaneData"          # inputs; see README "Data availability"
export METHANE_OUT_DIR="$HOME/MethaneData_outputs"   # outputs

Rscript setup.R      # reports R version, optional packages, resolved paths
Rscript run_all.R    # every stage listed in run_all.R
```

The core analysis needs **base R only** (R >= 4.0). Optional stages need `terra`,
`ncdf4`, `sf`, `png`, or `tigris`; `setup.R` reports which you are missing, and
`run_all.R` skips those stages with a message rather than failing. See the
dependency table in `README.md`.

Outputs land in `$METHANE_OUT_DIR` (CSVs, `paper_values.json`, `sessionInfo.txt`)
and `$METHANE_OUT_DIR/figures/` (PNGs). The small ones are also committed under
`results/` so the manuscript's numbers can be audited without the raw data.

The leg-block bootstraps (`york_boot()` in `R/ratios.R`, `.ols_block_ci()` in script 15)
each re-seed the RNG with 42 at the start of every call and restore the caller's RNG
state on exit, so a flight's interval depends only on that flight's data, not on the
order of scripts or on how many draws earlier flights consumed. `config.R` also seeds
42 at start-up for anything else that uses the RNG. Since 13 Sep 2026 the fossil
fraction is the within-leg (fixed-effects) York slope; `METHANE_FOSSIL_ESTIMATOR=pooled`
reproduces the pooled numbers of the original submission, and
`scripts/verify_paper_values.R` pins expectations for both.

There is **no closed-loop flight map**. No AMMBEC flight closes a valid box loop
(`13_closeloop_diagnostic.R`), so the manuscript reports no closed-loop flux and
the former loop figure was removed; the honest replacement is the loop-closure
diagnostic (Figure S1).

SI section S1.2 (`33_denver_curtain_screen.R`) tests the remaining possibility, a downwind
**curtain** rather than a closed loop, against the four conditions a curtain
requires (city entirely upwind of the wall, wall wide enough to span the plume,
vertical stack from near-surface to above the mixing height, and adequate steady
transport through the wall). It computes a flux only for a screen that passes all
four, so no curtain number can be quoted without the geometry that justifies it.

Scripts 16, 17 and 18 sum huge gridded inventories over the exact analysis box; their
scalar results are recorded in `config.R`. `run_all.R` re-runs 16 when the Vulcan file is
present; 17 and 18 are run by hand, so the main pipeline runs without the multi-GB archives.

## Ground platform (script 71)

- **NOAA ARL Air Resources Car (ARC)**, 13 days 24 June to 15 July 2024 (11 within the
  flight window): one `AMMBEC-ARL-Suite_ARC_<date>_R*.ict` per day, distributed as a
  per-day `.zip`, in `instruments/ARC` (`METHANE_ARC_DIR`; the default tries
  `<DATA_DIR>/ARC` and `<DATA_DIR>/../instruments/ARC`). Read by
  `scripts/71_arc_ground_ethane.R`, which unzips in a temporary directory, so the
  archives are used as downloaded. Carries Aeris CH4 + C2H6, Picarro G2401-m, G2201i
  d13C-CH4, NOx, O3, NH3, black carbon, PM and dGPS. PI Xinrong Ren; the files are
  preliminary revisions and their use requires the PI's agreement.
  Outputs: `arc_ground_plumes.csv`, `arc_ground_ambient.csv`, `arc_ground_ratios.csv`,
  `arc_ground_coverage.csv`, `arc_ground_summary.csv`, `figures/FigS15_arc_ground.png`.

## Conventions shared across scripts

- **Boundary-layer samples.** Wherever aircraft samples (rather than level legs) are
  selected as boundary-layer air, the cutoff is `BL_AGL_MAX_M` = 1500 m above ground
  (`config.R`; scripts 19, 24, 25, 54, 59).
- **Mixing height.** The Dalek 2 Doppler lidar at Boulder (`METHANE_LIDAR_DIR`). Its record
  has a gap from 28 June 04 UTC to 1 July; for the one flight in that gap (29 June L1, the
  curtain screen of script 33) the PUMAS Doppler lidar, in the DJ Basin that afternoon (`METHANE_PUMAS_DIR`),
  is used and labelled `lidar (PUMAS)`. The two series are never mixed otherwise.
- **CDPHE inlet delay.** Each reading is moved back by the measured delay (21 s CAT,
  17 s EMU) and given the GPS position logged at that earlier time (`R/read_mobile.R`).
- **Wind directions** are averaged on the circle (vector mean, or circular median in
  script 24), never with `mean()` or `median()` of degrees.
- **Units.** Emission rates are t/h (t CH4/h).

## Refreshing `results/`

After a full run, `bash refresh_results.sh` copies the curated outputs from the output
folder into `results/`, so the committed copies match the manuscript.

## Stages deliberately excluded from `run_all.R`

- **Run by hand, outputs consumed by run_all stages.** 43 (Carbon Mapper catalogue snapshot,
  read by 44 and 54), 57 (GRA2PES v1.1/v2.0beta per-cell sums, read by 58, 59 and 23),
  64 (HRRR-STILT exposure, Figure S15; needs the HRRR archive), 66 and 69 (ethane/methane
  lag and precision sensitivity, Figure S8). Re-run them before `refresh_results.sh` if their
  inputs change; otherwise `results/` keeps their last outputs.

- **05 (curtain flux) and 09 (summary merging those fluxes).** No flight supports
  a valid closed-loop or curtain flux, so these results are not reported. The
  scripts are retained for transparency rather than deleted, but excluded from the
  reproducible run so rejected numbers are never regenerated.
- **16, 17, 18, `make_metro_outline.R`.** One-time preparation, as above.
- **07 (NOAA CSL Mobile Laboratory, 9 July 2024) and 77 (its route overlap with the
  ARL car).** The laboratory's 9 July drive was an instrument test: GPS and several
  instruments were not working well, the files were never finalized (revision RA),
  and it carried no ethane. On the instrument team's advice the data are not used in
  the paper. The scripts (and `R/read_mobilelab.R`) are retained for reference only.

## The manuscript document

The Word manuscript and SI are maintained separately from this repository and are
**not** built by it. The pipeline's role is to emit every quantity they quote:
`results/paper_values.json` is the machine-readable digest of the headline values,
with the per-flight detail in `table1.csv`, `ratio_method_flux.csv`,
`emission_estimates.csv`, `beta_sensitivity.csv`, `beta_endmember_ARC.csv`,
`qc_robustness.csv` and the other files under `results/`. Any number in the
manuscript should be traceable to one of those files via the table above.
