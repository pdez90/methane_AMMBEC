# Reproducibility map — AMMBEC Denver urban-methane paper

Every quantitative claim in the manuscript is regenerated by the R pipeline in
this repository. Run from the **project root** (the directory holding `config.R`):

```bash
export METHANE_DATA_DIR="$HOME/MethaneData"          # inputs; see README "Data availability"
export METHANE_OUT_DIR="$HOME/MethaneData_outputs"   # outputs

Rscript setup.R      # reports R version, optional packages, resolved paths
Rscript run_all.R    # every stage listed in run_all.R
```

The aircraft and ground-car stages need base R only (R >= 4.0). Reproducing every number
in the paper also needs `ncdf4` and `terra` with the gridded EPA GHGI file (stage 14 feeds
the required stage 21; stages 13 and 53 read NetCDF). Other optional stages need `sf`,
`png`, or `tigris`; `setup.R` reports which you are missing, and
`run_all.R` skips those stages with a message rather than failing. See the
dependency table in `README.md`.

Outputs land in `$METHANE_OUT_DIR` (CSVs, `paper_values.json`, `sessionInfo.txt`)
and `$METHANE_OUT_DIR/figures/` (PNGs). The small ones are also committed under
`results/` so the manuscript's numbers can be audited without the raw data.

The leg-block bootstraps (`york_boot()` in `R/ratios.R`, `.ols_block_ci()` in script 15)
each re-seed the RNG with 42 at the start of every call and restore the caller's RNG
state on exit, so a flight's interval depends only on that flight's data, not on the
order of scripts or on how many draws earlier flights consumed. `config.R` also seeds
42 at start-up for anything else that uses the RNG. Since 13 Sep 2026 the fossil
fraction is the within-leg (fixed-effects) York slope; `METHANE_FOSSIL_ESTIMATOR=pooled`
reproduces the pooled numbers of the original submission, and
`scripts/verify_paper_values.R` pins expectations for both.

## Conventions shared across scripts

- **Boundary-layer samples.** Wherever aircraft samples (rather than level legs) are
  selected as boundary-layer air, the cutoff is `BL_AGL_MAX_M` = 1500 m above ground
  (`config.R`; scripts 19, 24, 25, 54, 59).
- **Mixing height.** The Dalek 2 Doppler lidar at Boulder (`METHANE_LIDAR_DIR`). Its record
  has a gap from 28 June 04 UTC to 1 July; for the one flight in that gap (29 June L1, the
  curtain screen of script 33) the PUMAS Doppler lidar, in the DJ Basin that afternoon (`METHANE_PUMAS_DIR`),
  is used and labelled `lidar (PUMAS)`. The two series are never mixed otherwise.
- **CDPHE inlet delay.** Each reading is moved back by the measured delay (21 s CAT,
  17 s EMU) and given the GPS position logged at that earlier time (`R/read_mobile.R`).
- **Wind directions** are averaged on the circle (vector mean, or circular median in
  script 24), never with `mean()` or `median()` of degrees.
- **Units.** Emission rates are t/h (t CH4/h).
- **Aircraft background.** `add_enhancements()` runs the rolling 5th percentile (300
  samples) over each flight's urban legs joined end to end (basin legs for the basin
  ratio), so near a leg boundary the window spans the neighbouring leg. The along-track
  alternative (background on the continuous flight record) is reported in SI S4: median
  25% instead of 29%, and two CH4:CO flights would fail r >= 0.7. Script 24 (facility
  scan) uses a flight-wide 5th percentile only to locate plume samples near the complex.
- **Endmembers.** 0.102 is adopted; 0.0813 (2024 production-area ratio), about 0.10 (2021
  basin mass balance, Daley et al.) and 0.110 (Denver delivered gas) are regional
  reference values (medians 27 to 36%). 0.061 in script 52 is an assumed lean-basin
  scenario only, not a literature value.

## Level legs: constant-altitude transects (8 Oct 2026)

Every analysis script gets its level legs from `detect_level_legs()` (`R/massbalance.R`).
Since 8 Oct 2026 that function returns **constant-altitude transects** (`R/transects.R`),
found from GPS altitude, ground track and position only, with no trace-gas variable:
GPS altitude within ±50 m of the transect's running median, ends trimmed where the
altitude is more than 25 m from the median, rejected if the least-squares altitude trend
exceeds 0.25 m/s, 20-s smoothed ground track within 30° of the mean of its first 10 s,
gaps up to 20 s bridged, at least 60 s and 2 km, and neighbouring transects joined when
they are under 30 s apart and within 15° and 50 m. Each transect is then clipped by
region: each contiguous run of it inside the urban box, the basin (north of 40.05°N) or
neither is one unit, so only in-box samples enter the urban fits, and the leg-block
bootstrap resamples these units.

The original detector (20-s smoothed avionics `Vertical_Speed` ≤ 1) is kept as a
sensitivity: `METHANE_LEG_METHOD=vspeed`. That field, like the DGPS `Heave`, runs about
six times the rate of change of GPS altitude (r ≈ 0.99 over 30 s; on the 3 July L1
climb, +1,613 m by GPS against 8,946 by integrating `Vertical_Speed`), so its nominal
1 m/s threshold was about 0.17 m/s and split continuous transects at small altitude
corrections. `scripts/verify_paper_values.R` holds the expected values for both
definitions. `scripts/88_transect_maps.R` writes `transects.csv`, `transects_vs_legs.csv`
and one map plus altitude-time panel per flight (`figures/transects/`). The STILT runs
(script 64) were made for the original legs and have not been repeated.

The Word manuscript and SI are maintained separately from this repository and are
**not** built by it from scratch; the numbered Python scripts `scripts/47..98_*.py`
(with `scripts/docx_edit_lib.py`, Python 3 and `python-docx`) apply the scripted,

## Mixing height: airborne Doppler lidar (10 Oct 2026)

The mixing height used by the in-boundary-layer filter (script 22, Table S3), the curtain
screen (script 33, Table S2), the hotspot crossing rates (script 59, Table S10) and Table S1
(scripts 13, 90) is the boundary-layer height measured below the Twin Otter by the CSL airborne
Doppler lidar MD02 (`AMMBEC-AirborneDL-BLH_TwinOtter_*_R1*.ict`, read by `R/airborne_blh.R`;
put the files in `$METHANE_DATA_DIR/AirborneDL_BLH/` or set `METHANE_AIRBORNE_BLH_DIR`).
`flight_mixing_height()` returns the median of the in-box retrievals during a flight (the whole
track when fewer than three lie in the box); script 22 uses the retrieval at each sample,
interpolated along the track; script 59 uses the retrievals within 5 min of each crossing.
Five flights have no airborne file (28 June, 3 July L2, 11 July L2, 12 July L1 and L2); for
them the ground lidar at the DSRC (Dalek 2; PUMAS on 29 June) is used. Under strong westerlies
the DSRC vertical-velocity variance picks up turbulence aloft not connected to the surface
(S. Baidar), and it exceeds the airborne value on 10 of the 13 flights covered by both.
`METHANE_BLH_SOURCE=ground` reproduces the ground-lidar results as a sensitivity.
`scripts/91_transects_vs_blh.R` places every urban transect relative to the airborne boundary
layer (`transects_vs_blh.csv`) and tabulates both mixing heights per flight
(`airborne_blh_flights.csv`). The fossil fractions and CH4:CO emission estimates have no
mixing-height term and do not change.
